Gyojin Han (한교진)

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Information & Electronics Research Institute, KAIST, supervised by Junmo Kim. I am also actively involved in the InnoCORE AI Meta-Scientist Research Group, advised by Tae-Kyun Kim. I received my B.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Electrical Engineering from KAIST in 2021 and 2026, respectively.

My research focuses on visual generative models and 3D/4D computer vision, particularly at their intersection: generating, editing, and retrieving 3D objects, dynamic shapes, and human motion. More recently, I have been extending this line of work toward multimodal large language models, with an emphasis on reasoning and agentic systems. Earlier in my Ph.D., I also worked on AI safety, including model inversion and data poisoning attacks.
