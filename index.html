Gyojin Han (한교진)

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Statistical Inference and Information Theory (SIIT), KAIST, supervised by Junmo Kim. I received my B.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Electrical Engineering from KAIST in 2021 and 2026, respectively.

My research interests lie in the broad fields of deep learning, specifically focusing on Generative Models, 3D/4D Computer Vision, AI Safety, and LLM Reasoning.
